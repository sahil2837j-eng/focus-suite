YouTube Focus settings

One shared brain: these lists and the on-device classifier drive the on-page filtering and how Focus Suite classifies the videos you actually watch (waste / educational / 18+). One keyword per line, or comma-separated. Settings sync across your Chrome profile.

Allowed — always shown

Wins over everything, including Blocked and Only useful mode. Use it for false positives (failsafe vs fail) and shows you love.

Good topics — Only useful mode allows

In Only useful mode these (plus Allowed and classifier-approved learning) are shown. Also counted as educational when the tracker classifies a video.

Blocked — hidden in Balanced and Only useful

Hidden from results, home, subscriptions, channels and playlists. Also counted as waste by the tracker's 40-minute entertainment rule.

Smart content classifier

Runs locally on the video title, channel, and URL — no network, no video download. It recognises education, memory training, self-improvement, entertainment, and 18+ material. Education, memory, and self-improvement pass; entertainment is hidden in Balanced and Only useful; unknown videos are hidden in Only useful.

18+ is always hard-blocked. Turning the classifier off disables category-based filtering, never the 18+ block. Keyword mode still applies. Known adult domains are also blocked at the network layer.

Search override

A search that matches the right list skips filtering for that results page.

Matching rules

  • Case-insensitive. Left word boundary only: focus matches “focusing”, art does not match “smart”.
  • Multi-word keywords match spaces or hyphens: geo tv matches “geo-tv”.
  • Special characters are safe: 18+, c++, node.js.
  • Allowed always wins over Blocked.
